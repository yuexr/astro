I got frustrated with trying to grow pretty roses around my base. I had spent like an hour or two completing Fault Finders and traded them in for a full medium canister of Hybrid Rose seeds, yet when I planted them, none of them turned out to be the one I wanted.

_What terrible luck!_, I thought. _1 out of 8 chance to get the variant I want, and nothing?!_

Upset and full of spite, I created this.

It wasn't until much later that I found out that:

- Hybrid rose seeds aren't exactly randomized, the seed itself cycles through the variants visually and you can time your planting to try to target the variant you want.
- Someone in the Astroneer discord told me I could've just saved my file & reset if I didn't get the variant I want.

Sometimes I am not very smart. Alas.
